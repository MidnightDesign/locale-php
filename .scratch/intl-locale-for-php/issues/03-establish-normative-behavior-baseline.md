# Establish the normative behavior baseline

Type: research
Status: open

## Question

What is the complete current `Intl.Locale` semantic surface in ECMA-402 and Test262, which algorithms and Unicode/CLDR data it depends on, and which observable requirements have changed across recent spec, Test262, ICU, and CLDR revisions?

Produce a linked Markdown research asset that inventories constructor/options behavior, canonicalization, getters, `maximize()`/`minimize()`, locale-information methods, test counts/categories, normative data dependencies, and version-sensitive expectations. Recommend a reproducible spec and Test262 pin, but do not choose the product’s compatibility promise on the user’s behalf.
